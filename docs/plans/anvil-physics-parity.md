## Binary acceptance

Every reported state is finite. Sparse impulses affect only the addressed body. Zero-gravity unconnected and welded assemblies conserve linear momentum within 0.5% and do not gain more than 1% kinetic energy without external work. Offset centres of mass and rotated inertia frames are tested explicitly. Gravity-scaled velocity agrees with the analytic value within 1e-4 m/s. Paired bodies share an axial impulse according to their current masses within 0.5%. Connections below the reference impulse threshold survive; above-threshold linear/angular loads generate events, independently of time step and solver setting. A zero event threshold is not treated as unbreakable. Replacing/reordering a body or joint cannot retain an invalid native joint or misattribute a contact. Geometric anchors and compound offsets follow PhysicsWorld.Scale.

The reference implementations must first pass their analytic contract checks: a nonmoving or uninitialized reference is not an oracle. A/A means two separately created simulations, not parsing one report twice. Contact/constraint differences are measured in metres, radians, kg m/s and N s; no bitwise equality between distinct solvers is required. Solver tolerance is declared per case, not relaxed after seeing a failure without a logged decision.
